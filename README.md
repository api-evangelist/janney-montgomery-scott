Janney Montgomery Scott LLC is a full-service wealth management, financial services, and investment banking firm headquartered in Philadelphia, Pennsylvania, and continuously operating since 1832.

A wholly owned subsidiary of The Penn Mutual Life Insurance Company, Janney serves individual investors, corporations, and municipalities through more than 100 branch offices across the United States, with over 900 financial advisors managing in excess of $167 billion in client assets under advisement.

**No public developer API or machine-readable specification has been documented at this time.** Janney offers the MyJanney client portal and iOS/Android mobile apps for account access and reporting but does not publish a general-purpose developer API or external developer portal.
